# Commercial Contracting Playbook (Synthetic)

> Synthetic teaching playbook. Not legal advice.

## Limitation of Liability

Standard position: mutual cap equal to 12 months of fees paid or payable. Carve-outs from the cap are required for confidentiality breaches, data protection breaches, indemnification obligations, and gross negligence or wilful misconduct. Red flag: a cap below 12 months of fees, a one-sided cap that only protects the provider, or a cap that applies to data protection breaches. Escalate to senior counsel if the cap is below 6 months of fees.

## Indemnification

Standard position: mutual indemnities; the supplier must indemnify us for third-party intellectual property infringement claims. Red flag: one-way indemnity running only from us to the supplier, or no IP indemnity from the supplier.

## Termination

Standard position: mutual termination for material breach with a 30-day cure period. Termination for convenience must be mutual. Red flag: supplier-only termination for convenience, or a cure period longer than 30 days for us to exit.

## Term and Renewal

Standard position: auto-renewal is acceptable if the non-renewal notice period is 60 days or less. Red flag: notice period longer than 60 days, or automatic price increases above 5% on renewal.

## Payment Terms

Standard position: payment within 45 days of invoice receipt; late interest no higher than 1% per month. Red flag: payment terms shorter than 30 days or late interest above 1% per month.

## Data Protection

Standard position: security measures meeting ISO 27001 or SOC 2, and breach notification within 72 hours. Red flag: vague standards such as commercially reasonable security, or notification within a reasonable time without a fixed deadline.

## Non-Solicitation

Standard position: non-solicit of employees limited to 12 months, with an exception for general job advertisements. Red flag: non-solicit longer than 12 months or a no-hire clause without a general solicitation exception.

## Governing Law

Standard position: New York or Delaware law. England and Wales is acceptable for UK counterparties. Red flag: any other jurisdiction requires review.

## Confidentiality

Standard position: mutual obligations, reasonable care, standard exclusions, survival of 3 to 5 years. Red flag: survival under 2 years or missing standard exclusions.
