# Home Sleep Apnea Testing (Synthetic Medical Policy)

> Synthetic teaching document. Not a real payer policy and not medical advice.

## Scope

Unattended home sleep apnea testing (HSAT) for adults with suspected obstructive sleep apnea (OSA).

## Criteria for approval

HSAT is medically necessary when the patient has a high pretest probability of moderate-to-severe OSA, documented by a STOP-BANG score of 3 or higher, or by snoring plus witnessed apneas or excessive daytime sleepiness (Epworth Sleepiness Scale above 10).

## When in-lab polysomnography is preferred

HSAT is not appropriate, and attended in-lab polysomnography should be requested instead, for patients with significant cardiopulmonary disease, neuromuscular disease, suspected central sleep apnea, chronic opioid use, or suspected non-respiratory sleep disorders.

## Documentation required

STOP-BANG or Epworth score, relevant comorbidities, and current medications including opioids.
