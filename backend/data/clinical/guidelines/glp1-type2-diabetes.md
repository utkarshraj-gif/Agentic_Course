# GLP-1 Receptor Agonists for Type 2 Diabetes (Synthetic Medical Policy)

> Synthetic teaching document. Not a real payer policy and not medical advice.

## Scope

This policy covers GLP-1 receptor agonists and dual GIP/GLP-1 agonists when prescribed for glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus. Use for weight management alone is covered under a separate policy (MP-RX-230) and is out of scope here.

## Criteria for approval

Initial approval for 12 months requires ALL of the following: a documented diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus (ICD-10 E11.x); HbA1c of 7.0% or higher within the last 90 days; and a trial of metformin for at least 3 months at a maximally tolerated dose, OR a documented contraindication or intolerance to metformin.

Step therapy is waived for patients with established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease or chronic kidney disease, where a GLP-1 agent with proven cardiovascular benefit is preferred.

## Exclusions

Type 1 diabetes. Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or multiple endocrine neoplasia type 2 (MEN2). Concurrent use of two GLP-1 agents. History of pancreatitis requires prescriber attestation of risk review.

## Renewal

Renewal for a further 12 months requires documentation of an HbA1c reduction of at least 0.5 percentage points from baseline OR achievement of the individualized HbA1c goal, and continued adherence.

## Documentation required

Diagnosis code, most recent HbA1c value and date, metformin trial dates and dose or reason for contraindication, and screening for thyroid carcinoma history.
