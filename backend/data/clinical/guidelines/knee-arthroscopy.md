# Knee Arthroscopy with Meniscectomy (Synthetic Medical Policy)

> Synthetic teaching document. Not a real payer policy and not medical advice.

## Scope

Arthroscopic partial meniscectomy of the knee (CPT 29880, 29881) in adults.

## Criteria for approval

Arthroscopic meniscectomy is medically necessary when ALL of the following are met: mechanical symptoms such as locking, catching or giving way; a meniscal tear confirmed on MRI; failure of at least 4 weeks of conservative treatment including physical therapy, activity modification and analgesics; and absence of advanced osteoarthritis (Kellgren-Lawrence grade 3 or 4) on weight-bearing radiographs.

A locked knee that cannot be reduced is an urgent indication and bypasses the conservative therapy requirement.

## Not medically necessary

Arthroscopic debridement or lavage for knee osteoarthritis. Meniscectomy for degenerative tears without mechanical symptoms in patients with moderate-to-severe osteoarthritis.

## Documentation required

Description of mechanical symptoms, MRI report, radiograph grade, and conservative treatment history with dates.
